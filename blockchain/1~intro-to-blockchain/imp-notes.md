Point 5: Inflation Hedge

Inflation matlab paise ki value kam hona. Example: aaj ₹100 mein jo samaan aata hai, 10 saal baad wahi samaan ₹200 mein aata hai. Tumhare ₹100 ki taqat aadhi ho gayi.

Gold ki supply limited hai, sarkar isse chhaap nahi sakti. Toh jab paise ki value girti hai, gold ki keemat (rupees mein) aam taur pe badhti hai. Isliye gold lamba time ke liye “purchasing power” bachaake rakhta hai.

Example: 1980 mein ek tola gold se jitni cheezein kharid sakte the, aaj bhi lagbhag utni hi kharid sakte ho, bhale rupees mein keemat bahut zyada ho gayi ho. Rupee ki value gir gayi, gold ki taqat nahi.

Point 6: Independence from Governments

Rupee, dollar jaisi currencies sarkar aur bank ke bharose pe chalti hain. Agar sarkar fail ho jaye ya bank doob jaye, toh us paise ki value bhi gir sakti hai.

Gold kisi desh ya sarkar ka nahi hai. Har desh mein uski value hai. Toh jab logon ka bharosa sarkar, bank ya currency se uthne lagta hai, woh paise ko gold mein badal dete hain, kyunki gold ko koi “band” ya “bekaar” nahi kar sakta.

Example: Agar tumhare desh mein economic crisis aaye aur currency crash ho jaye, tab bhi gold dusre desh mein bech ke paise mil jaate hain.

Short mein: Paise ki value sarkar ke haath mein hai, gold ki nahi. Isliye mushkil time mein log gold pe bharosa karte hain.

Note: “Historically” word important hai. Yeh har time guarantee nahi hai, kabhi kabhi gold ki keemat gir bhi sakti hai.
